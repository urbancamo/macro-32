# G7 -- Part I Audit Checklist

> Generated 2026-07-12 by the `scave-g7-audit` workflow (10 read-only agents, one per
> spec section). Each solo SC-row of engine-spec.md Part I mapped to the MACRO-32 port
> with a verdict + file:line evidence. **The reference engine wins; vectors are never edited.**

**317 rows audited.** Verdict tally: gap 164, conformant 136, ui-pending 9, discrepancy 7, na-multiplayer 1

## Key finding

The headless engine `ENGINE.MAR` is a **vector-driven subset**: it implements exactly the
rules the 8 conformance vectors exercise (movement, chamber draw, Earthquake, the reduced
1-front fight + gang-up/Spectre, Lost-Ruby statue, crossings, scoring). The remaining
~160 `gap` rows are rules the vectors never hit -- the TEST/reaction layer, all 8 artifacts,
the Eye of God, openChest, moveTreasure/drop/setBorne, the multi-front fight model, and the
RNG hazards (Medusa/Ghouls/Mutiny/Trap effects). Most exist in the **legacy interactive
modules** (STRANGER/ARTIFACT/FIGHT/SPECIAL) that `SCCONF` does not link -- and several of
those legacy paths carry their own defects the audit flagged (see notes). So the game ships
the rule, but not in the engine, and not verified. G8 ('no game fact computed in UI code')
requires porting these into `ENGINE.MAR`; most need new conformance vectors to verify.
