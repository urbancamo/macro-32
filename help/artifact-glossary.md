# Artifact Glossary

Most treasure is simply worth points -- silver, gold, and gems. The
twelve artifacts below also carry special powers. An artifact's power
only works once an exploring party has actually taken it up.

## Magic Sword

A man, woman, or hero bearing the magic sword may fight spectres
hand-to-hand, which is otherwise impossible. It also adds to fighting
strength.

## Magic Carpet

Transports the party one level directly up or down, or to a card
adjacent along an edge. A new area card may be placed if necessary. If
the party encounters strangers it may not withdraw. If carried to the
viper pit or deep pool, the party ends up on the island.

## Lotus Dust

May be used before approaching strangers, or before any round of
fighting, to weaken the enemy.

## Healing Balm

May be applied only at the beginning of a turn in which the party is not
involved in a fight.

## Talisman

It has no warding-off power until actually taken up by an exploring
party. The party possessing it may use its power to protect other
parties in the same area.

## Strength Potion

May be taken immediately before any round of fighting to boost a
fighter for that round.

## Magic Staff

Medusa cannot harm a wizard bearing the staff. A wizard's magical power
is increased while carrying it.

## The Ring

The bonus value of the ring applies to all die rolls in a round of
fighting, even if the bearer is slain in that round. On the fourth level
or deeper, die rolls which would normally indicate the bearer's death
are ignored.

## Lost Ruby

If a party arouses the statue guarding the ruby and then retreats, the
statue attacks any party which later enters the area.

## Charmed Flute

Dragons and vipers are lulled to sleep immediately and remain asleep as
long as the party is in the area. The flute-player may still fight in
the same turn. A dragon may be put to sleep before strangers are tested,
so a friendlier creature becomes their leader.

## Eye of God

A powerful gem. Priests, wizards, and all other artifacts are powerless
while it is in the same area; spectres and zombies are permanently
destroyed. Once taken up, the Eye must be kept with the main body of the
party, or a curse falls on that party until it is taken up again. You
are never obliged to pick up the Eye.

## Treasure Chest

Heavy -- two or more creatures may join in carrying it. It can be opened
at the beginning of any turn while still in the Cave. If a spectre
appears it attacks with magical power 5 and the turn ends after one
round of fighting; if not defeated it stays and is hostile to all.
