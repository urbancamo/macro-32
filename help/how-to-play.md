# How to Play

Welcome, adventurer. The Sorcerer's Cave is a solitaire game of
exploration. You lead a small party of creatures down into a cave of
many levels, fighting monsters, gathering treasure, and trying to climb
back out alive with as high a score as you can.

## Building your party

At the start you have 6 selection points to spend. Each creature you may
recruit costs some of those points; stronger creatures cost more. Use
the Up and Down arrows to move the highlight, and press Enter (or Space)
to add the highlighted creature. The number keys 1-8 add a creature
directly. The roster line shows exactly who is in your party so far.
Press 0 when you are done. You need at least one creature.

## Moving through the cave

Each turn your party occupies one area -- a tunnel or a chamber. The
status line shows the level, area, and which exits are open. Move with
the direction keys:

- N, E, S, W to travel through a doorway in that compass direction.
- U and D to climb up or down a staircase, when one is present.

The arrow keys are accepted as alternatives to N/E/S/W. Press M at any
time to open the cave map, which shows every area you have discovered.

## Chambers, strangers, and fights

When you enter a chamber you may find strangers (other creatures) and
treasure. Strangers must be tested: they may prove friendly and join
you, stay indifferent, or turn hostile and force a fight. Treasure in a
chamber is shared out among your party only once every creature there
has joined you or been defeated -- if any remain indifferent, the
treasure stays put.

Fights are resolved in rounds, matching your fighters against the enemy.
Magic, artifacts, and sheer fighting strength all matter. You may
sometimes retreat through a secret door.

## Hazards

Some chambers hold hazards. A trap drops the party to the level below
unless you can counter it. An earthquake can collapse an area. Medusa
turns the unprepared to stone. Ghouls force a fight. A deep pool may
make you leave heavy treasure behind in the doorway -- you can reclaim
it by leaving the pool the same way you came.

## Winning

Your aim is to gather treasure and defeat creatures for points, then
return to the Gateway on level 1 and climb out. Quitting or dying ends
the game and scores you on what you carried and accomplished. Press the
[?] key during play at any time to return to this manual.
